Why it's arranged this way

Density only works if the eye knows where to go.

Left column is what you owe. Your list, then what's waiting on Connie, then every project. It reads as "my work, our work, the whole slate."
Right rail is what's happening. Meetings, momentum, the room, the board — glanceable, none of it demanding action. A rail is the right home for things you check rather than do.
The top three inches decide everything. Greeting, two-week strip, the nearest project. If someone reads nothing else, they still know what day it is and what's closest.

The move: build this as one page, in the order shown. The strip, hero, your list and the counts I can do from data that already exists — that's most of the page and roughly a day and a half. Meetings and the Milanote board each need one small field on the project. Momentum reads timestamps we're already storing. Nothing here needs a new system, only assembly.
